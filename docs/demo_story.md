# 🎤 ChurnSense Hackathon Demo Story

Use this narrative to wow the judges during your demo!

## 1. The Problem (The Hook)
"Every year, businesses lose billions to customer churn. But the real tragedy isn't just the loss—it's that most companies don't know *why* it's happening or *how* to stop it in time."

## 2. The Solution (The Intro)
"Introducing **ChurnSense**: An AI-powered retention intelligence platform. We don't just predict who will leave; we explain the 'Why' and provide the 'How' to keep them."

## 3. The Demo Flow (Step-by-Step)

### Step 1: The Executive View
- Start at the **Executive Dashboard**.
- Show the **Revenue at Risk** ($10K+). "This isn't just a number; it's the financial impact of inaction."
- Point to the **Risk Distribution**. "We've categorized our customers into risk tiers automatically."

### Step 2: Operational Intelligence
- Navigate to **Customer Risk Table**.
- Sort by **Priority Score**. "We use a smart ranking (Churn Prob × Customer Value) so your team knows exactly who to call first."

### Step 3: The Deep Dive (The 'Wow' Moment)
- Search for a customer in **Customer 360 Profile**.
- Show the **Key Churn Factors**. "Our engine explains the behavior—low engagement combined with recent refund issues—and recommends an exact retention action."

### Step 4: Proactive Strategy
- Open **Campaign Simulator**.
- Run a simulation for 'High Risk' with a 'Discount'. "Before we spend a dollar, we simulate the ROI. We predicted a 25% retention rate and $2k in protected revenue."

### Step 5: Enterprise Reporting
- Show the **Reports & Export** page. "Everything we see here is ready for Executive Reporting through our built-in **Power BI integration**."

## 4. The Closing
"With ChurnSense, we turn churn from an inevitable loss into a measurable growth opportunity. Thank you!"
